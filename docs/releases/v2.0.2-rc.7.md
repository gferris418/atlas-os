## Summary

ATLAS OS 2.0.2-rc.7 integrates ARGUS `3.0.0-rc.1` as the governed government commercial opportunity intelligence subsystem while preserving the Collection-Need Gate and ATLAS decision authority.

The candidate expands structured collection beyond tender/procurement intelligence to support early government need and capability-gap discovery, legitimate opportunity development, supply-first technology/OEM discovery, supplier partnerability and matching, event-driven early warning, installed-base expansion and Rapid Tender recovery.

## Routing changes

New ARGUS collection semantics include:

- `NEED_GAP`
- `POLICY_DRIVER`
- `PROGRAMME_PROJECT`
- `APPROVAL_STATUS`
- `SUPPLIER_PROFILE`
- `REFERENCE_PROJECT`
- `EXPORT_FEASIBILITY`
- `PARTNERABILITY`
- `OPPORTUNITY_DEVELOPMENT`
- `OPPORTUNITY_MATCHING`
- `EXPANSION_DISCOVERY`
- `RAPID_TENDER`

Early need/gap discovery, legitimate pre-procurement opportunity development and supply-first technology matching may activate ARGUS without automatically activating `atlas.procurement-intelligence`. Procurement plans, formal tenders/acquisitions, eligibility/evaluation work, bid/no-bid and `RAPID_TENDER` retain the ATLAS Procurement Intelligence capability.

## Commercial opportunity boundary

ARGUS may now return:

- persistent pre-PX opportunity intelligence;
- supplier/OEM intelligence and partnerability;
- opportunity-supplier match assessments;
- advisory opportunity qualification recommendations;
- detection metrics and early-warning events;
- evidence, conflicts, gaps and collection coverage.

ATLAS continues to own:

- canonical evidence acceptance;
- authoritative opportunity priority;
- `PROMOTE_TO_PX`;
- pursuit and bid/no-bid decisions;
- external-action authorization;
- persistence authorization;
- forecasting/scenario conclusions;
- assurance and final delivery.

The governing rule is: **ARGUS may calculate and recommend; ATLAS authorizes and decides.**

## Access Asia identity model

A persistent early opportunity may exist as `ARGUS-OPP-YYYY-NNNNNN` before a commercial PX identity exists. ATLAS assigns `YYYY_PXNNNNN_SHORTNAME` only through governed promotion or explicit commercial-pipeline registration. The ARGUS identity remains linked to the PX lineage through pre-award and post-award lifecycle moves.

Tender publication alone does not assign a PX identity or imply submission state.
