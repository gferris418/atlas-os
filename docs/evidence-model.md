# Evidence model

Evidence classification is semantic, not a confidence shortcut. A verified claim has adequate provenance and an executed check. Supported claims have useful but incomplete evidence. Inferences are derived, assumptions are temporary inputs, speculation is possible but weakly evidenced, and unknown means the system cannot evaluate the claim.

Source quality, completeness, confidence, and inference are recorded separately. Source precedence is task-sensitive but generally favors user-authoritative material, connected authoritative systems, official primary sources, canonical project sources, strong secondary sources, community material, and model prior knowledge.

Evidence health summarizes required coverage:

- `GREEN`: required evidence is verified without material conflict.
- `AMBER`: usable evidence has managed gaps or uncertainty.
- `RED`: required evidence conflicts, fails, or is materially insufficient.
- `UNKNOWN`: health cannot yet be assessed.

Missing evidence never closes a control or becomes evidence of absence.
