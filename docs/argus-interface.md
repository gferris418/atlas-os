# ARGUS Interface

ARGUS is the ATLAS-governed **360° Business Intelligence playbook** exposed through the `intelligence_collection` mechanism. It is not a peer operating system and it is not the underlying search, MCP, plugin, database, or connected-source provider. ATLAS decides whether ARGUS is needed, authorizes the mission, and selects or authorizes the actual capabilities/providers used to execute it.

```text
ATLAS
  -> approved bounded intelligence mission
  -> authorized capabilities/providers/tools
ARGUS
  -> applies 360° BI doctrine and collection/analysis method
  -> IntelligenceCollectionPackage
ATLAS
  -> reassess / verify / reroute / analyze / authorize action / deliver
```

## ATLAS owns

- whether ARGUS is needed;
- explicit activation approval;
- mission objective and scope;
- permissions and constraints;
- capability and provider selection/authorization;
- canonical evidence acceptance;
- authoritative commercial decisions;
- action and persistence authorization;
- the next step after ARGUS returns.

## ARGUS owns inside the mission

ARGUS applies its intelligence playbook across materially relevant business-intelligence lenses, including demand, supply, opportunity, stakeholder, competitor, procurement/tender, market, partnerability, post-award context, and commercial-performance intelligence when supported by evidence.

Its collection method remains:

`PIR -> EEI -> Collection Plan -> Source Plan -> Collect -> Resolve -> Grade -> Competing Hypotheses -> Gaps -> Package -> Learn`

Once authorized, ARGUS may adapt its lawful collection and analysis path within scope without routine callbacks. It may not expand scope, alter permissions, self-activate, choose providers outside ATLAS policy, self-route after return, submit bids, accept project milestones, issue invoices, record accounting transactions, execute payments, or make the final ATLAS/business decision.

Operational business systems may expose facts to ARGUS for intelligence and analytics. Those facts do not transfer transactional execution authority to ARGUS.
